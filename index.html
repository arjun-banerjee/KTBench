KTBench
leaderboard runs repo

KTBench evaluates whether an LLM can translate a GPU kernel from one DSL and hardware target to another, correctly and efficiently. A run gives a model a working implementation in a source DSL (PyTorch, CUDA, Triton, NKI, ...) on a source GPU, and asks it to produce an equivalent implementation in a target DSL on a target GPU. The harness runs a six-stage pipeline against a diverse correctness suite and a randomised stress suite, then measures performance as a fraction of the target hardware's ceiling.

The leaderboard aggregates published runs by model. Success rate is the fraction of runs whose submission cleared the structured correctness gate plus the stress threshold. Mean final score is correctness × stress_pass_rate × sol_score, the headline KTBench number. Mean SOL is the Speed-of-Light fraction — achieved_throughput / hw_peak — bounded by physics rather than by a runtime baseline, so it cannot be inflated by slowing the reference. The second table breaks both down by translation axis (source DSL → target DSL). A held-out re-verification is not in the score itself; KTBench's anti-hack defence is the diverse + random-shape stress suite plus the utilization gate, and traces from each cell are linked from the runs index for direct inspection.

Column glossary

runs
Number of published submissions in the group. A submission is one (model x problem x persona x scenario shape) cell.
success rate
Fraction of runs whose final outcome was passed (correctness gate cleared, stress gate cleared, utilisation above the no-op floor). The remaining runs split between failed (a submission landed but a gate rejected it) and incomplete (the model exhausted its turn budget without calling submit_kernel).
final / mean final
The headline KTBench score: correctness_rate × stress_pass_rate × sol_score. Always in [0, 1]. A no-op kernel that passes a hardcoded structured suite scores ~0 because the stress suite and the SOL term zero it out. A correct, robust, hardware-saturating kernel approaches 1. The "mean final" column averages this across the runs in the group.
SOL / mean SOL
Speed-of-Light fraction: max(achieved_throughput / hw_peak_throughput) across the compute and memory axes. The hardware peak comes from the per-device entry in src/ktbench/registry/hardware.py (e.g. H100 SXM: 989 TFLOP/s FP16, 3350 GB/s DRAM). SOL is bounded by physics, not by a runtime baseline, so it cannot be inflated by slowing the reference. mean SOL is the arithmetic mean over the group.
speedup / mean speedup
Ratio of the candidate's mean runtime to the handwritten reference_tgt.py baseline on the same inputs. Reported but not part of the gated score because it can be inflated by shipping a slow reference. mean speedup is the geometric mean over the group so a single 100x outlier does not dominate.
gpu-seconds
Sum of accelerator wall-clock attributed to the runs in the group. Excludes scheduler idle time and LLM-side latency; counts only the eval pipeline's actual GPU work (compile, structured cases, stress, timing).
axis
Translation axis as <src_dsl> → <tgt_dsl>: the language the candidate translates from and to. Hardware (A100/H100/H200) is implied by the per-problem meta.toml.
outcome
Per-run terminal state. passed = submit landed with a non-zero final score; failed = submit landed but a gate (correctness, stress, utilisation) zeroed the score; incomplete = the agent never reached submit_kernel before the turn budget or scheduler quiescence stopped the run.
